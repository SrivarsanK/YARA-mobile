Do not center the extension on:
The best SIH extension is one that feels broader than the bus MVP, while still being explainable and demoable.
It adds a clear network-level intelligence story.•
It avoids overcommitting to complex live train integrations.•
It gives judges a believable scale-up path.•
Constraints and risks to make explicit
Rail route maps can mislead if too distorted.1.
Interchange logic must be simple and clear.2.
Coach-level crowding claims should stay approximate unless well supported.3.
The extension should not depend on complex live railway integrations.4.
Static network guidance may matter more than live train telemetry in the demo.5.
